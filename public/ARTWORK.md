# Menu Artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, then exported as small WebP
assets with transparency preserved. These illustrations represent menu types,
not exact photographs of every dish. Custom menus use the same type-based mapping.

Files: `menu-rice.webp`, `menu-noodles.webp`, `menu-sweet.webp`, `menu-drink.webp`.

Prompt template:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset: isolated food artwork for a cute romantic
> Thai meal picker. Subject: [subject below]. Style: charming clean hand-painted
> gouache, delicate charcoal outlines, subtle paper grain, sophisticated cozy food
> illustration, muted rose and sage with natural bright food colors. Front
> three-quarter perspective, square composition, one centered food arrangement
> fills 75 percent of the canvas, fully visible with generous transparent margins.
> No text, no characters or faces, no logos, no hearts or extra decorative symbols,
> no background, no seafood. Genuine transparent background.

Subjects:
- Rice: a mint-green shallow ceramic bowl of Thai chicken basil rice with a fried
  egg, basil leaves and rice clearly visible.
- Noodles: a rose-pink shallow ceramic plate of spaghetti carbonara with bacon,
  curled golden noodles and a little parsley.
- Sweet: a small mint-green plate with one strawberry cake slice and one butter
  toast slice.
- Drink: one clear iced lemon tea glass with visible amber tea, ice cubes, lemon
  slice and a mint-green straw, a small lemon wedge beside it. Drink prompt used
  "one centered drink arrangement" and "muted rose and sage accents" instead of
  their food-template equivalents.
